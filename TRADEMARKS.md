## Guidelines for Forks and Redistribution

You are fully encouraged to fork neoOMSI and distribute modified builds in accordance with the GNU General Public License.

### Development and Contribution Forks

Forks created solely for contributing changes back to the official neoOMSI repository may retain the neoOMSI name and branding, provided they remain clearly associated with the upstream repository and are not presented or distributed as an independent version of neoOMSI. The rebranding requirements below apply when a fork is maintained, published, packaged, or distributed as a separate project or product.

### Independent Redistribution and Rebranding Requirements

To prevent confusion among players and community members, redistributions of modified code must adhere to these requirements:

1. **Distinct name:** Your fork or redistributed package must use a distinct name that cannot be confused with the official neoOMSI project. Do not use "neoOMSI" as the primary title of your fork.
2. **Distinct branding:** You must remove or replace the official neoOMSI logos and application icons with your own visual assets.
3. **No implied endorsement:** Do not state or imply that your fork is official, endorsed, or affiliated with the neoOMSI core team.

## Permitted Uses

- Truthful, non-commercial editorial use (e.g. news articles, videos, streaming, tutorials, blog posts).
- Indicating compatibility for community add-ons and mods (e.g. "Compatible with neoOMSI").
