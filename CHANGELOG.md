# Changelog

All notable changes to neoOMSI will be documented in this file.

Releases and versioning follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) (see
[docs/RELEASING.md](docs/RELEASING.md)); binary downloads are available on the
[Releases](https://github.com/neoOMSI/neoOMSI/releases) page.

## 0.2.3 - 2026-10-27

### Fixes

- Fixed multiplayer map downloads failing when another program uses the host's TCP ports. (!636)
- AI buses no longer jam stations or run red exits; cars pass, pull out and park without grazing. (!645)

### Changes

- Updated the five loading screen backgrounds.
- Server code and build inputs changed since 0.2.2.

## 0.2.2 - 2026-10-25

### Fixes

- `GetHeightAbovePoint` detects overhead wires on coupled vehicle parts and AI vehicles. (!625)
- Timetable buses keep their vehicle for the next scheduled trip, even after a very short trip. (!631)
- AI vehicles no longer stop permanently when their route crosses buried ground surfaces, as at Sprackel on Städtedreieck. (!632)
- AI trams and trains no longer stop in bends because another rail vehicle is on the adjacent track. (!633)
- Spline-attached objects, such as bus shelters, now match OMSI placement on joined and older maps. (!634)
- Vehicles with `[registration_list]` followed by `[registration_automatic]` now use the listed plates, as in OMSI 2. (!635)
- The pause menu keeps regular weather choices available when METAR sync is off. (!637)
- Fullscreen recovers when the swapchain stops presenting: failed resizes are retried, with a borderless fullscreen fallback. (!642)
- Trams backing over two-way stubs and switches stay on the rails instead of stopping or breaking apart. (!644)

## 0.2.1 - 2026-10-25

### Fixes

- AI buses serve a stop before following their track onto the lane beside. (!611)
- Navigator follows the route from where the bus was teleported instead of leading back. (!612)
- Indicator toggles now latch on buses with a comfort indicator, such as the MAN NLC. (!613)
- AI vehicles no longer sink tilted into the ground while a map tile streams in. (!614)
- Keys pressed while the pause menu is open no longer move the camera behind it. (!617)
- AI trams keep to 50 km/h on unposted track; AI rail vehicles run up to their own top speed. (!619)
- Overhead wires on track splines no longer pull the ground up into walls. (!620)
- AI trams no longer block their track for good at the edge of the loaded map area. (!622)
- Kajosoft Citaro Facelift vehicles no longer spawn with active electrics when autostart is disabled. (!626)
- The outside tracking camera no longer collapses to the origin or locks zoom on long buses such as the O560 III 15m. (!627)
- Rear boarding now also lets riders who stamp a ticket board articulated buses at the back. (!629)

### Performance

- Compressed textures are cached in memory and on disk; reloads skip re-encoding. (!618)

### Internal & tooling

- Linux builds link with mold by default. (!615)
- Fix the release branch's test build after the !611 backport. (!623)
