Completed the traffic-AI refactor's Stage 1 and Stage 2. The traffic domain now runs the same fixed 20 ms decision tick in the window and offscreen paths, with an end-to-end frame-partition replay test. AI vehicles use stable `VehicleId`s and typed stop constraints instead of raw `u64`s and string hints. The road network carries `[crossingproblem]` and typed `[blockpath]` rules, ignores vertical separation when finding conflicts, validates degenerate and ambiguous content with diagnostics, and reports explicitly whether a route is complete, still waiting for tiles, or invalid. Stop berths compile into typed boarding targets with their route occurrence.
