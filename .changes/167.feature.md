Multiplayer players can now talk to each other in the game without any extra program: voices come from where each player stands, fade with distance and sound muffled through closed bus doors. Push-to-talk (CapsLock, Shift+CapsLock for the radio) or voice activation, a microphone test with a level meter and per-player volumes live under Options > Sound > Voice chat, and hosts and server admins can mute players or turn voice chat off.
