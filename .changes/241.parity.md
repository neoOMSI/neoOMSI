Tunnel portals, junctions and other objects laid with "align terrain" now cut the ground as OMSI 2 does: the ground ends on the object's terrain hole rim at the rim's height, so no hill blocks the road in front of a tunnel (Städtedreieck's Kaiserberg tunnel) and buses drive through. Objects without the flag no longer cut the ground, and the flag no longer cuts a strip out under an unrelated road. Trees now turn their crossed cards the way OMSI 2 does, so a tree beside the Kaiserberg tunnel no longer reaches into it, and a tunnel spline no longer opens a slot in the hill above it.
