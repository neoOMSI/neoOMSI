The navigator's map no longer slows the game down after a map has been started once: its ground is kept between sessions and is ready seconds after starting. Building it the first time takes about half as long and costs less frame rate, and its road markings no longer change from one session to the next on maps that use textures of the same name from different add-ons.
Higher frame rates on slower computers at crossings built of many roads, curbs and markings, such as Städtedreieck's main station or the Sprackel crossing: the roads are drawn with about half as many draw calls, by day and at night. The picture is unchanged.
