AI cars no longer drive into bus lanes the map keeps free of random traffic ("no non-timetable traffic", e.g. at Städtedreieck's Magdalenenkrankenhaus): they move over to the open lane in time, or wait for a gap before the bus lane, and at a junction they keep off a road that goes on only as a bus road (#125). AI cars no longer drive round and round inside junction objects whose short paths lie close together (#265, Städtedreieck's Gehrenstraße; #311, Cayuga). AI emergency vehicles on a call (ambulance, police, fire brigade) no longer wait out red lights: crossing traffic stops for them, the cars in front of them roll carefully over the line, and they keep their right of way while queueing. On a road with one lane each way they pass the queue in front of them on the oncoming lane, across the junction ahead when it is reserved for them, and the oncoming traffic stops before the stretch they need (#462). AI buses no longer stand for good behind passengers waiting at the kerb where they pull out of their bay: after a few seconds they edge past at walking pace, and the waiting passengers step aside (#338).
