AI cars no longer drive into bus lanes the map keeps free of random traffic ("no non-timetable traffic", e.g. at Städtedreieck's Magdalenenkrankenhaus): they move over to the open lane in time, or wait for a gap before the bus lane, and at a junction they keep off a road that goes on only as a bus road (#125). AI cars no longer drive round and round inside junction objects whose short paths lie close together (#265, Städtedreieck's Gehrenstraße; #311, Cayuga). AI emergency vehicles on a call (ambulance, police, fire brigade) no longer wait out red lights: crossing traffic stops for them, the cars in front of them roll carefully over the line, and they keep their right of way while queueing. On a road with one lane each way they pass the queue in front of them on the oncoming lane, across the junction ahead when it is reserved for them, and the oncoming traffic stops before the stretch they need (#462). AI buses no longer stand for good behind passengers waiting at the kerb where they pull out of their bay: after a few seconds they edge past at walking pace, and the waiting passengers step aside (#338). AI buses, articulated ones above all, pull up far enough into a short bay to leave the road beside it free instead of standing with their rear across it at the pole, so the traffic behind no longer queues for as long as they stand there (Gerolstein station). On motorways, AI cars no longer keep right onto an acceleration lane only to queue at its end to merge back; cars coming from the on-ramp move over onto the motorway as soon as there is a gap, and without one wait at the end of the lane beside the road instead of standing in the way of the traffic going past, and where an acceleration lane or a lane that drops out runs into the road it gives way to the traffic on it (Gerolstein's motorway). An AI car that has already nosed into a roundabout no longer stops there for the traffic coming round behind it, and no longer waits for good for a bus that stopped behind it on the ring because of it. AI vehicles keep to their path through tight turns, as in OMSI, instead of running out wide over the kerb after them, and the turning radius in their files (`[inv_min_turnradius]`) no longer holds them off a path laid tighter (#495, buses leaving Gerolstein's depot). AI cars changing lanes keep their distance to the player's bus and no longer cut in right in front of it. A car that has pulled over for an emergency vehicle which cannot get past it drives on after a few seconds and clears the way, instead of both waiting for each other. An AI emergency vehicle passes a queue while the oncoming traffic stands waiting for it, instead of waiting for those cars to come past first. It also passes on roads whose two lanes are drawn a little further apart, and while it waits at a junction beyond the queue.
 Traffic turning into the oncoming side of a junction now waits for an emergency vehicle while it passes its queue there, a car in front of it crosses a red light for it only when it goes the same way, and oncoming traffic stops for it only where it can actually pass. AI cars no longer weave after a lane change at motorway speed. Vehicles no longer meet head-on in a single-lane stretch driven both ways under traffic lights (Ahlheim's tunnel): an emergency vehicle crossing against its red light there waits for the car coming through towards it. At a junction whose signal stands a few metres before the crossing, the car in front of an emergency vehicle crosses the red light for it again, and an emergency vehicle that gave up waiting there no longer keeps the junction reserved, which held the cross traffic for minutes. A car on a lane that left the road shortly before and runs back into it, as after a short widening, gives way where it merges; a timetable bus coming out of its bay keeps its way. An emergency vehicle also passes a queue on the oncoming lane up to a junction where it turns off, and turns from there.
